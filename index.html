Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to worldlings@mndz.ai. Tell us your device and the level, if it's about a puzzle.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud save, so uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

How do I play?

Place one Worldling in every row, every column and every color region. Worldlings never touch, not even diagonally. Every puzzle has one solution you can reach by logic alone.

Hints

Every puzzle comes with one free hint, and each world gives you one extra. A hint shows where to look, explains why, then makes the move.
