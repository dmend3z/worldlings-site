Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

Worldlings ("the app") is published by Daniel Mendes, established in Portugal ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: worldlings@mndz.ai.

2. The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The app sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to our analytics provider, PostHog. They are linked to a random identifier that the app creates, not to your name. We call this data "pseudonymous": it does not tell us who you are, but it is still treated as personal data.
  • There are no purchases. The app does not ask for permission to use your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
  • We do not ask for the iOS tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so the app cannot access your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data stored only on your device

The app keeps a small local database on your device with:

  • the levels you completed, with the time taken, the number of hints used and the completion time;
  • your daily puzzle results;
  • puzzles in progress (your moves, the elapsed time and the hints used), so you can continue later;
  • your settings and game state (for example language, sound, accessibility options, tutorial status and hint credits).

We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app has no cloud save or sync. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Android backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings.

4. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (PostHog)

What is sent

  • Gameplay events: app opened; tutorial started and completed; puzzle started, completed or abandoned; hints requested and used; creatures and worlds unlocked; daily puzzle started and completed.
  • Event details: level, world and creature identifiers; difficulty; time spent; number and type of hints; the daily puzzle date.
  • Technical context: app version and build configuration; a random session code created at each launch; a random identifier that the PostHog software creates and keeps on your device; the operating system and its version; the device type (phone or tablet); your device's language and region setting; your time zone; screen names inside the app; the app state (foreground or background).
  • App lifecycle: when the app is opened (including the link that opened it, if any), comes back to the foreground or goes to the background.
  • Crash and error reports: unexpected errors with technical details (error message and stack trace), and short technical log messages (for example "start-up completed", or "puzzle started" with the puzzle identifier and difficulty).
  • IP address and approximate location: every connection to PostHog reveals your device's IP address.
    • PostHog uses the IP address to estimate your approximate location (such as country and city) and adds it to the events.

      The app never collects your precise (GPS) location.

What is not sent: your name, email address, phone number or any account; your contacts or photos; your precise location; your advertising identifier (PostHog never receives it); your individual taps on the puzzle board. There is no screen recording. We do not link this data to your identity: the app never tells PostHog who you are.

Why: to understand how the game is played, find levels that are too hard, fix crashes, and check whether features work.

Provider and location: PostHog, Inc. processes this data on our behalf as our service provider (processor). The data is hosted in the United States (US) region. Data is therefore transferred outside the EEA, under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement.

Retention: PostHog keeps events for 1 year and then deletes them.

Your choice: this version of the app has no setting to switch statistics off. You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. If you reinstall it, a new random identifier is created.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Gameplay statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in running, improving and securing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Data stored only on your device: this data is not sent to us. It is needed for the game to work.

6. Who receives data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • PostHog, our analytics provider, which processes it on our behalf;
  • authorities, if the law requires it;
  • a buyer or successor, if the app or our business is transferred. This policy will continue to apply.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing or a copy of it (portability). You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, withdraw a consent you gave, and complain to a data protection authority (in Portugal, CNPD, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados). Write to worldlings@mndz.ai.

Because we don't know who you are and the identifier is random, we may not be able to find your statistics unless you can give us that identifier. To delete the data on your device, uninstall the app.

8. Children

Worldlings is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not ask for anyone's age. The pseudonymous technical data described above is collected in the same way for every player. We do not knowingly collect any other personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact worldlings@mndz.ai.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The data on your device is protected by your operating system's app isolation.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date are always at https://dmend3z.github.io/worldlings-site/privacy/. If we make important changes, for example a new data type or a new provider, we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact
